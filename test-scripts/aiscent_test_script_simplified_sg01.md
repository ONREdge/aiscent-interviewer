# AISCENT Test Script — SG-01 (Pre-Climb Early Exit)

Before you start:

- Refresh `/aiscent` in the browser (fresh session).
- Click **Start the interview** and allow the mic.
- Read your lines below in order.

---

**Agent:** Hi, thanks for making time. I'm going to walk you through a short interview — about twelve to fifteen minutes — that looks at six areas of customer experience at your organization. I want to focus on what currently exists today, not what's planned or in progress. There are no right or wrong answers here, just what's actually working. Ready to get started?

**Tester:** Yes, ready.

**Agent:** Great. Let's start with leadership. Tell me about leadership's involvement in customer experience at your organization — things like executive ownership, governance, and how CX priorities show up in business decisions.

**Tester:** Honestly, no one owns CX at our organization. Different functions do their own thing. There's a line in our values about caring for customers, but there's no governance.

**Agent:** You didn't mention whether CX has influenced budget or resource decisions — has any budget or priority ever changed because of CX governance at your organization?

**Tester:** No. Nothing like that has happened.

**Agent:** Thanks for being straight with me — that gives me what I need. I'm going to stop the interview here rather than walk through the other areas, because the read is already clear. Give me one moment while I pull it together on screen.
