# AISCENT Test Script — HP-01 (Full Happy Path)

Before you start:

- Open `/aiscent` in the browser.
- Click **Start the interview** and allow the mic.
- Read your lines below in order.

---

**Agent:** Hi, thanks for making time. I'm going to walk you through a short interview — about twelve to fifteen minutes — that looks at six areas of customer experience at your organization. I want to focus on what currently exists today, not what's planned or in progress. There are no right or wrong answers here, just what's actually working. Ready to get started?

**Tester:** Yes, ready.

**Agent:** Great. Let's start with leadership. Tell me about leadership's involvement in customer experience at your organization — things like executive ownership, governance, and how CX priorities show up in business decisions.

**Tester:** Our COO owns customer experience. We have a governance council that meets monthly. Their decisions have shifted our budget priorities — we reallocated onboarding budget last year based on the council's recommendation, and product changed their roadmap in response.

**Agent:** That's helpful. Is the executive ownership of CX documented anywhere — defined metrics, accountabilities, decision rights?

**Tester:** There's a charter document that defines the council. We haven't fully documented every decision right yet, but every function has changed behavior at some point because of council decisions.

**Agent:** Got it. Now let's move to your operations. Does your organization have a customer journey map? Tell me about it — how it was built and how it gets used.

**Tester:** Yes, we have a journey map. It was put together by our operations team a couple of years ago — mostly maps out our process steps end-to-end.

**Agent:** You didn't mention how it was built — was it based on customer research and interviews, or more from your internal process documentation?

**Tester:** It came out of a workshop where each function walked through what they do. We didn't interview customers for it.

**Agent:** And how do your listening programs or health tracking connect to the map — are those anchored to specific journey stages?

**Tester:** Not really. Our CSAT program runs separately, health tracking is another tool. The map is more of a reference doc.

**Agent:** Understood. Walk me through how your organization listens to customers — the sources you use, how they connect, and what you hear between formal surveys.

**Tester:** We anchor our surveys to key moments — within 48 hours of onboarding completion, right after a support case closes. We've started transcript analysis on our support calls. And signal from support is starting to reach product regularly.

**Agent:** How does all of that connect — is there shared infrastructure that ties the sources together, or is each one still on its own platform?

**Tester:** We're on a shared analytics platform now — everything flows into it, though we're still building out which teams have direct access.

**Agent:** Good. How does your organization track customer health — your ability to see risk forming before a customer flags a problem?

**Tester:** We have a health score for every account. It combines survey sentiment with behavioral data — login frequency, feature adoption, support ticket volume.

**Agent:** How predictive is it — does the model flag accounts before the customer has indicated a problem, or does it confirm problems that are already underway?

**Tester:** It updates when the behavior shifts, so it flags accounts before the customer complains. But usually by then the drift has already been happening a few weeks.

**Agent:** Makes sense. When something is going wrong across your customer base — a pattern, not just one account — how does your organization see it, prioritize it, and get someone accountable for responding?

**Tester:** Our CX team runs a monthly cadence — signal in, prioritization based on customer impact and health risk, action, resolution tracking. Leadership can see what we shipped and what it moved. It's a real rhythm, not ad hoc.

**Agent:** How quickly would your team see a systemic pattern forming — is the integration of signal and health data automated, or does someone assemble it on a schedule?

**Tester:** It's manually assembled — someone on the CX team pulls it together for the monthly review. Latency is a few weeks.

**Agent:** Last one. Does your organization proactively intervene in customer journeys based on signals — before customers flag a problem? Tell me how that works.

**Tester:** We piloted a proactive intervention program on our renewal journey — it uses health signals to trigger CS outreach before the renewal window opens. The CX team coordinates each intervention manually. We haven't scaled it to onboarding or the other journeys yet.

**Agent:** Is that running across your full customer base and all key journeys, or is it concentrated in that renewal area?

**Tester:** Just renewal for now. Scaling would mean building out the routing logic for every other journey — we haven't started that.

**Agent:** Thanks for walking me through all of that. I'll pull your read together on screen now — one moment.
